### Field Communications Summary - Dandelion Africa

#### 1. Practical Actions Completed:
- Conducted a family planning outreach event at Place0 dispensary, serving 42 participants.
- Held a menstrual hygiene session for 60 girls in classes six to eight at Place5.
- Organized a community dialogue on gender-based violence involving 35 women and some village elders at the chief’s camp in Place3.
- Conducted a cervical cancer screening camp at Place1, screening 28 women and referring 3 for further treatment.
- Led a kitchen garden training for 18 women farmers at Place0.

#### 2. Blockers & Technical Issues:
- Ran out of contraceptive implant stock during the family planning outreach at Place0, resulting in six referrals to another facility.
- Long queues at both the cervical cancer screening camps due to high demand, leading to discomfort among participants.
- Rainfall affected attendance and comfort levels during the family planning outreach at Place0.

#### 3. Outcomes & Resolutions:
- Confirmed strong interest in future events as indicated by requests for return visits.
- Established connections between victims of gender-based violence and counselors through the community dialogue.
- Distributed reusable sanitary pads to all attending girls during the menstrual hygiene session.
- Sent detailed reports of screenings to relevant parties post-event.
- Planned follow-up visits to check progress on kitchen gardens initiated during the training sessions.

#### 4. Pending Follow-ups:
- Schedule a return visit for the family planning outreach at Place0 to address the referral backlog.
- Coordinate additional resources such as chairs and water for upcoming screening camps to manage longer queues effectively.
- Confirm dates for subsequent meetings with the women’s groups in Place4 and Place5.
- Contact the head teachers and mothers discussed regarding menstrual hygiene education.
- Arrange follow-up consultations for the three women identified with abnormal cervical cancer screening results.
- Plan regular visits to monitor the development of kitchen gardens established during the training sessions.
